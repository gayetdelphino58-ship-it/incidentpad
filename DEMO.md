# IncidentPad — 60–90s demo script

**Goal:** Show filing an incident end-to-end and sharing the summary.

### 0:00–0:15 — Hook (Landing `/`)

> “When something goes wrong on the road, drivers lose time on calls and texts while dispatch waits for a clear picture. IncidentPad files an incident in under two minutes and gives you a shareable summary.”

Point to problem / solution cards, then click **Start new incident**.

### 0:15–0:45 — Wizard (`/new`)

1. **Type:** Choose **Breakdown**.
2. **Details:** Trip `LD-DEMO`, location `I-35 N, Exit 12 — rest area`, leave time as-is, short description (“Check engine + limp mode; parked safely; roadside ETA 45 min”), severity **Medium**, notify **Dispatcher & Shipper**.
3. **Checklist:** Tick “Truck stopped safely”, tap **Fake attach photo** (marks photos noted). Skip police if not needed.
4. **Review:** Glance rows, hit **Submit incident**.

### 0:45–1:10 — Summary (`/incidents/[id]`)

> “Here’s the shareable summary — one tap to copy for Slack, SMS, or email.”

Click **Copy summary** — button shows **Copied**, green banner appears. Optionally mention **Print** for paper/PDF.

### 1:10–1:30 — List & reset (`/reports`)

Open **Reports**: new incident at top plus five seed demos. Mention **Reset demo** restores seeds for the next judge.

**Close:** “No backend, mobile-first, under two minutes — IncidentPad for RoadStar 2026.”
